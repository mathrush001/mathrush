Math Rush — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026

The short version

Math Rush does not ask for your name, your email, or an account of any kind. We do not know who you are.

Your progress is saved on your device. The game sends anonymous usage and crash information to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and see which levels are too hard.

Stored on your device

Your level progress, star grades, coin balance and sound settings are stored on your device only. They are never uploaded.

You can clear them at any time with Reset Progress in Options, or by uninstalling the game.

Sent to Firebase

Analytics: which level was started, completed or failed, which gate you chose, how long a session lasted, and similar gameplay events. Alongside these, Firebase records standard technical information — device model, operating system version, country, language — and a randomly generated app instance identifier.

Crash reporting: if the game crashes or hits an error, a report is sent describing the fault and the state of the device at the time.

None of this identifies you personally, and none of it is sold.

Never collected

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details.

Advertising

This version of Math Rush shows no advertising. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Who else is involved

Google, as the provider of Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, processes the data described above on our behalf. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing and can be read at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Math Rush is not directed at children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Significant changes will be described in the release notes.
